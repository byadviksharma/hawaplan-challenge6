Data acknowledgements

Regional US AQI and particulate estimates are provided by Open-Meteo using
Copernicus Atmosphere Monitoring Service (CAMS) global forecasts. City-centre
coordinates were checked with Open-Meteo Geocoding using GeoNames data.
HawaPlan formats timestamps in India Standard Time, groups hourly samples
into periods and calculates ranges for its household plans.

The Copernicus Atmosphere Data Store publishes CAMS global atmospheric
composition forecasts (2021). HawaPlan accesses these forecasts through
Open-Meteo. https://doi.org/10.24381/04a0b097
Bulletins contain modified CAMS information for the year of the displayed
forecast. The European Commission and ECMWF accept no responsibility for
how the information in these bulletins is used.
